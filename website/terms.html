6. Public Health & Peer-Reviewed Reference Sources

Velda Fitness uses established public guidelines strictly as reference baselines for default daily nutrition targets and cycle regularity awareness notes. None of these algorithms diagnose any condition.

  • Nutrition Reference · U.S. Government
    Daily Value on the Nutrition and Supplement Facts Labels — U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA.gov) ↗

    Source for default adult daily upper reference limits: Sodium (2,300 mg), Added Sugars (50 g), and Cholesterol (300 mg).

  • Nutrition Reference · U.S. Government
    Dietary Guidelines for Americans — U.S. Department of Agriculture & Health and Human Services ↗

    Federal dietary guidance for daily calorie balance, protein/carbohydrate/fat distribution, sodium moderation, and added sugar limits.

  • Cycle Reference · U.S. Government
    Your Menstrual Cycle — U.S. Office on Women's Health (WomensHealth.gov) ↗

    Educational reference for menstrual phases (menstrual, follicular, ovulatory, and luteal), typical cycle variation, and signs that warrant talking to a doctor.

  • PCOS Awareness · National Institutes of Health (NIH.gov)
    Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) — Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute of Child Health and Human Development ↗

    Federal clinical overview describing Polycystic Ovary Syndrome, ovulatory irregularity, and the importance of professional medical evaluation.

  • Peer-Reviewed Journal · PubMed
    The two FIGO systems for normal and abnormal uterine bleeding symptoms (Munro et al., 2018) ↗

    Published international reference parameters for adult cycles: 24–38 day frequency, cycle-to-cycle variation under 7–9 days, and bleeding up to 8 days.

  • International Clinical Guideline · PubMed
    2023 International Evidence-based Guideline for the Assessment and Management of PCOS (Teede et al., 2023) ↗

    Defines adult cycle irregularity thresholds (cycles shorter than 21 days, longer than 35 days, or over 90 days for any single cycle) used as non-diagnostic educational prompts in Velda.
