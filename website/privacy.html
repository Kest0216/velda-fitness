Privacy Guarantee

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026 · Operator: Velda Fitness · Contact: support@veldaapp.com

Velda Fitness is built around a simple promise: your health journal belongs strictly to you. We do not operate developer servers, cloud user accounts, crash trackers, advertising networks, or analytics collectors. We cannot read, sell, or share your workouts, meals, recipes, or menstrual cycle logs because we never receive them.

1. Stored on Your iPhone

All workouts, custom exercises, body weight logs, nutrition records (calories, protein, carbs, fat, sodium, sugar, and cholesterol), saved recipes, dish photos, and optional cycle logs live directly on your iPhone. Unsaved recipe search results clear automatically after 60 days so your storage stays tidy.

2. Your Private Apple iCloud & Keychain

  • iCloud Backups: When enabled in Apple Settings and Vault, backups sync directly into your own private Apple iCloud storage. No developer or third party has access.
  • Apple Keychain: If you connect OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic for optional smart features, your sign-in tokens or keys are stored encrypted inside your personal Apple Keychain.
  • What Is Shared When You Use AI Features: Optional AI features ask for your confirmation before running and send only the prompt you entered (such as a recipe search, dish title/ingredients for an image, recipe text/link for import, or meal description) straight from your phone to the provider you chose, under that provider's own privacy policy. Your full health history and cycle logs are never sent unless you intentionally export and share them yourself.

3. Export & Complete Erasure

In the Vault tab, you can export any section or your entire journal anytime—or confirm Erase All Data to permanently wipe local entries, photos, and saved credentials from the device.

4. Health Information Notice

Cycle phase colors, variation alerts, and nutrition targets are informational self-tracking tools only, based on public government and medical guidelines listed in our Health & Dietary Sources. They are not medical advice or a substitute for seeing a doctor.

5. Contact & Source Inspection

You can inspect every line of app source code in our public GitHub repository ↗, or reach us at support@veldaapp.com.
