Do I need an AI account to use Velda Fitness?

No. Logging workouts, body weight line graphs, daily calories and nutrients, manual recipes, dish photos from your camera or photo library, and cycle tracking all work completely offline with zero AI setup. An AI provider key or ChatGPT sign-in is only needed when you choose to run optional smart tools (web recipe search, AI recipe import, AI meal nutrition estimation, AI routine building, or generating a dish illustration). Note that Anthropic (Claude) handles text and recipes, while generating dish images requires OpenAI or Google Gemini.

How do I connect ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude?

Open the Vault tab in the app and scroll to Your AI Provider. Pick ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini, or Claude (Anthropic). For ChatGPT you can either sign in directly with your ChatGPT account or paste a key; for Gemini and Claude, paste your key. Tap Test Ping to check that it works—when the connection succeeds, the button turns green and saves it inside your Apple Keychain.

Where is my data backed up?

Everything stays right on your iPhone first. Inside Vault → Your Apple iCloud, you can sync to your private Apple iCloud account or save a backup file to iCloud Drive. You can also export any single section or your full journal anytime.

How do nutrition trends and cycle phase tracking work?

In the Macros tab, tap Trends next to Targets to view how your calories, protein, carbs, fat, sodium, sugar, or cholesterol change over time. In the Cycle tab, logging flow days highlights your estimated phases right on the calendar and gently flags noticeably irregular cycles based on published women's health guidelines.

You can inspect all official government and medical journal sources—alongside our non-medical disclaimer—on the Health & Dietary Sources page.

Can I hide the Cycle tab?

Yes. Open Vault → Preferences → Cycle Tracker Tab and switch it Off to hide it completely without losing past entries.

What does the $4.99 one-time purchase include?

Velda Fitness is a one-time $4.99 App Store purchase with zero subscriptions, zero ads, and zero developer servers. Every workout, nutrition, recipe, and optional cycle tool stays yours on your iPhone and in your personal Apple iCloud, with periodic updates to keep everything running smoothly.
